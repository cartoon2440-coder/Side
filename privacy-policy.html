Privacy policy

How we collect, use, and protect information when you use NovaStock.

Privacy policy Refund & replacement Terms of service
Last updated: September 2026

Information we collect

When you place an order or contact support, we collect the information needed to fulfill that request — typically your name, email address, and payment confirmation details. We do not collect more than what's needed to process your order and provide support.

How we use your information

  • Order fulfillment: to deliver the product or service you purchased.
  • Support: to respond to questions, replacement requests, or issues with an order.
  • Communication: to send order confirmations and delivery updates. We do not send marketing email unless you opt in separately.

Payment information

Card and payment details are processed directly by our payment provider and are never stored on our servers. We retain only a transaction reference to match payments with orders.

Data sharing

We do not sell customer information. Data is shared only with the service providers necessary to process payments and deliver orders, each bound by their own confidentiality obligations.

Data retention

Order records are kept for as long as needed to handle replacement requests, disputes, or legal requirements, after which they are deleted or anonymized.

Your choices

You can request a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by contacting support. We'll respond within a reasonable timeframe once your identity is confirmed.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@novastock.example or through the contact page.
