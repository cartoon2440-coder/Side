Refund & replacement policy

What's covered, for how long, and how to request help with an order.

Privacy policy Refund & replacement Terms of service
Last updated: September 2026

Replacement window

Most products are covered by a 48-hour replacement window starting from the moment of delivery. If a product doesn't match its listed description or fails to work as described, contact support with your order number and we'll issue a replacement.

What's covered

  • Products that don't match the description on the product page.
  • Delivery failures caused by an error on our end.
  • Access or login issues reported within the replacement window.

What's not covered

  • Issues reported after the replacement window has closed.
  • Change of mind after a product has already been delivered.
  • Problems caused by misuse outside the product's intended purpose.

Refund eligibility

Refunds are considered case by case, primarily for orders that have not yet been delivered or where a replacement isn't possible. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.

How to request a replacement or refund

Message support through the contact page or live chat with your order number and a short description of the issue. Most requests are resolved within 24–48 hours.
